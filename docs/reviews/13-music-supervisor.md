## Questions
1. **Brief format (15.2):** mood, flavor, vocals, tempo, energy, avoid list. What fields would you add?
2. **Measurements:** tempo, vocals, loudness, speech-clash (300 Hz–3 kHz), energy curve. Which predict a good bed under a talking host? Anything better than speech-clash?
3. **Shortlist of 3:** how should candidates be explained so a non-musician can choose quickly?
4. **Signature track:** one track across the whole Giro series, or variations (a few tracks in the same family)?
5. **Edit points:** choosing the section of a song for a 50 s reel; aligning to downbeats; ending on a musical phrase rather than a fade.
6. **Mix:** levels under speech and on silent cards, ramps. Our rule is ≈ 15 dB below speech peaks. Right for phone speakers?
7. **Taste profile:** what should the Hub learn from ratings, and what should stay a human decision?
