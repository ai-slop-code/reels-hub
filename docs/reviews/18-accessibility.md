**Priority:** good to have

## Why you
Most people watch Reels muted, and the design system relies on one accent color (pink) and white text over changing footage. Captions and graphics should work for everyone.

## Questions
1. **Contrast:** white Monstera-Black captions with a pink highlight over warm kitchen footage and dark DJI shots. Minimum contrast rule the Hub should check automatically?
2. **Color:** pink as the only accent on navy maps. Safe for color-blind viewers? Should information ever rely on color alone?
3. **Caption timing:** minimum display time and reading speed for Slovak captions on phones.
4. **Sound tags and speakers:** [bracket] sound tags and "Ľuboš:" labels. Good practice for muted viewers?
5. **Instagram caption:** alt text for the reel? Emoji use?
6. **Hub UI:** keyboard navigation and contrast for the owner's daily use.
